Our multimodal algorithm now generates routes you’ve never seen before Historically, there has been unfair discrimination by traditional journey planning algorithms against mixing public transit options (metros, trains, buses) with car options (cabs, driving), keeping them as separate choices. At Citymapper we believe all transport modes should be free to mingle, and coexist together in cities! #freedom #makecitiesgreatagain Our SuperRouter (A to B algorithm) is now combining transit and cars to create new trip possibilities you never knew existed .
